# Stock footage

Ads 2 and 3 play stock footage behind their text. Put a clip here named after
the slot it fills, and the next render picks it up. It can be any length over
4 seconds, any orientation and any format ffmpeg reads (`.mp4`, `.mov`,
`.webm`, or a `.jpg` for a still). The renderer crops it to 9:16, makes it
black and white, darkens it and pushes in slowly, so colour clips are fine.
A slot with no clip shows the plain dark background.

| Slot | Used in | What to look for |
|------|---------|------------------|
| `bed` | 2 (hook, "She'll notice") | A couple in bed, moody light |
| `gym` | 2 ("Forget the abs"), 3 ("It's a muscle") | A man lifting or flexing in a dark gym |
| `car` | 2 ("Forget the car") | A sports car at night |
| `sheets` | 2 ("in bed"), 3 ("think about it…") | Rumpled sheets or a bedroom at night |
| `couple` | 2 ("Last longer"), 3 ("Better sex") | A couple embracing or laughing |
| `clock` | 3 (hook) | A clock or stopwatch ticking, close up |

Free sources that allow commercial use without credit: Pexels, Mixkit,
Pixabay, Coverr.

For the bedroom and couple slots, prefer shots where faces aren't clearly
recognisable. The people in a stock clip never agreed to appear in an ad
about sexual stamina, and some stock licenses forbid showing people in a
"sensitive" context.
