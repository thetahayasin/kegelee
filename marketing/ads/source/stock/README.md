# Stock footage

The ads play stock footage behind their text. A clip in this folder is named
after the slot it fills, and the next render picks it up. It can be any length
over 4 seconds, any orientation and any format ffmpeg reads (`.mp4`, `.mov`,
`.webm`, or a `.jpg` for a still). The renderer crops it to 9:16, makes it
black and white, darkens it and pushes in slowly, so colour clips are fine.
A slot with no clip shows the plain dark background.

Free sources that allow commercial use without credit: Pexels, Mixkit,
Pixabay, Coverr. Vecteezy's free license requires crediting Vecteezy unless
you have Pro.

For the bedroom and couple slots, prefer shots where faces aren't clearly
recognisable. The people in a stock clip never agreed to appear in an ad about
sexual stamina, and some licenses forbid showing people in a "sensitive"
context.
